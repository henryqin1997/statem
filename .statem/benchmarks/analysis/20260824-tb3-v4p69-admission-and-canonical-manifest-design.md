## Evidence owner

`1` and StateM `0`, but the pair was protocol-invalid because the aggregate
source-manifest SHA included the worktree basename. The same run also activated
a `structured-transformation` compact practice whose catalog entry was marked
`admitted: false`.

These are controller/identity defects. They are repaired from archived
manifests and selector receipts without resampling the task.

   value `statem`. Private local paths remain excluded from the public manifest
   and aggregate identity.

   match remains in the receipt with `activation_reason: unadmitted_shadow` but
   cannot inject compact obligations.
3. Only `algorithm_performance_compact` is admitted because it has the fresh
   positive `batched-eval-parity` k1 observation. Stateful, structured, and
   numerical practices remain shadow-only.
4. The runbook, state graph, solver prompt backbone, and detailed reviewer
   catalog are otherwise unchanged. The distinct adapter identity is
   `ziheng-yaxin-statem-codex-thin-family-v4p69-exp`.

## Admission boundary

This version is not score evidence and does not authorize a same-task CLI
rerun. It is eligible for a new untouched pair only after source bundle and
route receipts are frozen. A future positive result may admit its exact family;
it must not broaden unrelated triggers or activate reviewer roles.
