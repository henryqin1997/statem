# Simulation And Control Review

- **`frames_units_and_timing`**: Identify state variables, coordinate frames, units, sign conventions,
  integration scheme, contact/event semantics, and control update timing.
- **`technical_drawing_dimension_scope`**: For technical diagrams, bind every dimension to the entities
  selected by its witness or extension lines, arrowheads, leader endpoint, and angular arc. Explicitly
  distinguish side-to-datum angles from included symmetric angles, radius from diameter, and projected
  from true length. Reconstruct at least one independent orthographic or analytic projection from the
  bound annotations; do not validate geometry only against expectations generated by the same
  interpretation used to build it.
- **`invariants_and_discontinuities`**: Check conservation or boundedness invariants, discontinuities, zero-step and
  variable-step behavior, saturation, reset, and deterministic seeding.
- **`boundary_and_analytic_cases`**: Exercise boundary transitions and compare equivalent formulations or smaller
  analytic cases.
- **`stability_versus_semantics`**: Distinguish numerical stability from physically correct semantics.
